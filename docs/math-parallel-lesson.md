# Hai đường thẳng song song — lớp 7, học kỳ 1

Original Vietnamese lesson, organised in the existing six stages. Scope: angle positions, Euclid's parallel axiom, properties and converse criteria, perpendicular/parallel relationships, hypothesis and conclusion, proof with reasons.

## Instructional decisions

- Say **tiên đề Euclid**, not “tiêu đề”. Explain it is accepted, not proved by dragging.
- Number angles counterclockwise from the upper-right sector at both A and B. A3/B1 are alternate interior; A4/B1 and A3/B2 are same-side interior; matching indices are corresponding.
- Distinguish using parallelism to calculate from using angle relations to establish parallelism.
- Six lab rounds: identify a pair; rotate a line through M; vary the transversal; select a parallelism criterion; identify a conclusion; justify three proof steps.
- Eleven content blocks, including four worked examples. Thirty exercises: 2 foundation, 5 guided, 4 self-check, 19 extra (including 4 written proofs).
- Written proofs are self-reviewed against explicit criteria, never automatically marked correct by a text matcher.
- SVG and PDF use one geometry model. Unknown angle values are revealed only in solutions. Source identity checks suppress diagrams after a user changes the question.
- Deleted questions are not restored. In development, the two revised introductory blocks are refreshed from source, and the original A4/B2 foundation question is moved to guided practice and replaced with an opposite-angle review. Deleted or edited questions are preserved. The discovery activity introduces angle-pair terminology before asking students to identify it. The new lesson is available in the default library and added when absent in development.
